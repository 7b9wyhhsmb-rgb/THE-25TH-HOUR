# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user through approval to continue: React, TypeScript, Vite, and CSS. The result must remain a fully editable local frontend project.

## Users

Visitors evaluating and exploring an independent fragrance brand on desktop, including people browsing fragrance families, products, rituals, and brand stories before deciding whether to sample or purchase.

## Product Purpose

Create an editable desktop fragrance storefront and brand experience whose information architecture and interaction quality closely follow the supplied Aesop reference.

## Operating Context

Visitors move between editorial campaign imagery, fragrance discovery, product browsing, scent-family navigation, layering guidance, and brand or atelier stories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The implementation must be local, runnable, and editable.
- Navigation, carousels, product data, drawers, and the guided scent finder must work rather than being static screenshots.
- Page content and product records must be data-driven.
- Desktop is the primary acceptance surface; smaller widths must remain usable.
- The supplied brand name is THE 25TH HOUR / 第二十五小时. Commercial claims, prices, and unsupplied product records must remain clearly replaceable demonstration content.

## Brand Commitments

- The user explicitly selected https://www.aesop.com/ as the binding visual and interaction reference.
- The user confirmed they have the legal rights required to reproduce and use the relevant site elements and assets.
- The final source must be easy for the user to modify.

## Evidence on Hand

- Aesop's public website is the supplied reference.
- The user supplied the THE 25TH HOUR / 第二十五小时 brand name, six paired product image sets, category order, sizes, and prices for 香水、香氛、身体油与卸妆膏. No testimonials, certifications, or additional product claims have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Editorial storytelling and commerce should share one continuous experience.
- Interaction must remain restrained, legible, and keyboard accessible.
- Brand, product, copy, and media should be replaceable from centralized data and asset files.
- Missing commercial truth must never be presented as verified fact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion fallbacks, accurate control labels, and sufficient contrast.
